What is the
Weekly Brief?

The Weekly Brief is a recurring leadership email from BCG CEO Christoph Schweizer to colleagues across BCG worldwide. Across the 69-edition archive, issues arrived typically every two weeks—a median 14-day cadence—and translate consequential business and societal developments into implications for leaders.

The archive also includes 10 Greater China TMT Daily Intelligence Briefs from August 10–27, 2026. These daily updates add a short-window TMT nowcast and do not independently establish long-term trends. Trend conclusions are now triangulated against a curated library of reports from leading multilateral institutions, regulators, strategy firms, and capital-markets research teams.

Authoritative Research

Search a tightly curated collection of reports from leading multilateral institutions, regulators, strategy firms, and capital-markets research teams. Every card links to the publisher’s original report or official reading page.

A deliberately narrow source universe

External research is used to validate, challenge, or sharpen the conclusions drawn from the Brief archive. It does not replace the original leadership messages, and a single external report cannot by itself establish a structural trend.

Inclusion rule

Original research only; transparent methodology or primary data; globally recognized institution; complete public report or substantive official reading page.
