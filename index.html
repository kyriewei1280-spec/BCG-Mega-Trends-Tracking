What is the
Weekly Brief?

The Weekly Brief is a recurring leadership email from BCG CEO Christoph Schweizer to colleagues across BCG worldwide. In this archive, issues arrived typically every two weeks—a median 14-day cadence. Each note focuses on a consequential business or societal development, from AI, geopolitics, productivity, and capital allocation to sustainability and industry change, and translates it into implications for leaders.
