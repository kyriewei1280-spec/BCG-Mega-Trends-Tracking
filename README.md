# Weekly Brief Atlas

An independent bilingual editorial synthesis of 69 unique Christoph Schweizer Weekly Briefs spanning May 2023 through August 2026, supplemented by 10 Greater China TMT Daily Intelligence Briefs from August 10–27, 2026 and 52 authoritative external reports from 19 institutions. The research library includes the complete available U.S.-China Economic and Security Review Commission annual-report series: 2002 and 2004–2025. The homepage explains the two evidence channels—internal leadership emails and external authoritative research—and how they are analyzed separately before being triangulated into megatrends and industry implications.

## Publishing

This repository is a static website prepared for GitHub Pages. Serve the repository root from the `main` branch.

## Files

- `index.html` — website interface, interactions, and styles
- `weekly-brief-atlas-data.js` — cleaned 69-issue article archive and taxonomy mappings
- `tmt-daily-data.js` — cleaned 10-edition Greater China TMT Daily Brief archive used as a short-window signal layer in the TMT Industry Lens
- `authoritative-research-data.js` — curated report metadata, official source links, industry mappings, and megatrend mappings. The library is append-only and uses verified publisher-hosted PDF links wherever a stable public PDF exists.
- `brief-translations-data.js` — complete Chinese reading translations for the 69 English CEO Briefs and complete English reading translations for the 10 Chinese TMT Daily Briefs; original-language articles remain authoritative and available in the reader

## Archive policy

Monthly updates preserve every previously published Brief, Daily Brief, and authoritative report record. New items are appended; metadata, taxonomy tags, and broken links may be corrected, but historical records are not removed when a newer edition is published. Research links are PDF-first, with official reading pages retained only when no stable public PDF is available.

The 23 USCC editions are treated as one longitudinal institutional series. Repetition across editions demonstrates persistence within that institution's assessment; it is not counted as 23 independent confirmations of a trend.

Every monthly release must update English and Simplified Chinese together. New records cannot be published without their alternate-language title, introduction, and complete reading version. Analytical conclusions, navigation, filters, legends, and evidence explanations are maintained in both languages.

This is an independent educational synthesis and is not an official BCG publication.
